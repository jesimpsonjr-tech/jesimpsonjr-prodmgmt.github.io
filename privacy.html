Last updated November 12, 2026

This policy covers the personal tools John Simpson runs on this site's behalf, including the "rclone-m4" Google OAuth app. These tools are for John's own use and are not offered to the public.

What is accessed

The rclone-m4 app uses Google Drive access only to upload and read files in John's own Google Drive (job-search documents his own automation creates). It does not access any other user's data.

How data is used and shared

Data is used only to move John's own files between his computer and his Google Drive. It is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Storage and removal

Access tokens are stored only on John's own computer. Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions.
